# Commercial licence

The GameSaloon server is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later. If the
AGPL does not fit your situation, Etcito offers a commercial licence for the
server instead.

## When you need one

You probably want a commercial licence if you:

- modify the server, or embed it inside a larger product, and do not want to
  publish your changes under the AGPL;
- want to offer a modified GameSaloon as a hosted service without releasing its
  source to the users of that service;
- need contractual terms the AGPL does not give (warranty, indemnity, support,
  a version commitment).

## When you do not

- Running the unmodified server, for any purpose, including commercial: the AGPL is fine.
- Modifying the server and publishing your changes under the AGPL: fine.
- Building a game that connects to a server over WebSocket: your game is not a
  derivative work of the server and is not covered by the AGPL, whatever its
  licence. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).
- Using the MIT-licensed protocol documentation or client code: fine under the MIT.

## How to get one

Write to **contact@etcito.com** with a short description of your use. Contact
us for pricing; terms depend on the use case and are agreed in writing.

Etcito can grant these licences because contributors license their
contributions to Etcito under the [CLA](CLA.md).
